Export cost and usage data from the AWS Billing area for reporting and offline review.

#### Steps
1. Open **Billing and Cost Management** in the AWS console.
![overview](/images/2.2-MonitorCost/6-dataExport.png)
2. Go to **Cost Explorer** and set the date range and filters you need.
3. Group data by service, account, or tag depending on the report goal.
