Create your Amazon QuickSight account before building the cost dashboard.

#### Prerequisites
- You can sign in to an AWS account with permission to enable QuickSight.
- Billing is enabled for your AWS account.
- You already chose the Region for this workshop (use the same Region in later steps).

#### Estimated time
10-15 minutes

#### Steps
1. Open the Amazon QuickSight console in your target AWS Region.
![overview](/images/2.2-MonitorCost/1-QuickSight.png)
2. Choose **Sign up for QuickSight**.
![overview](/images/2.2-MonitorCost/2-QuickSight.png)
3. Provide account details and complete the signup process.
![overview](/images/2.2-MonitorCost/3-QuickSight.png)
![overview](/images/2.2-MonitorCost/4-QuickSight.png)
4. Setup is complete.
![overview](/images/2.2-MonitorCost/5-QuickSight.png)
